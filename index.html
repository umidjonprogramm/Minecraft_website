About Minecraft

Minecraft is a sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios. It allows players to build and explore virtual worlds made up of blocks.

Features:

  • Creative Mode: Build and create without limits.
  • Survival Mode: Gather resources and survive against monsters.
  • Multiplayer: Play with friends online.
  • Mods and Customization: Enhance your gameplay with mods.

About the Game

Minecraft was created by Markus "Notch" Persson and released in 2011. It has since become one of the best-selling video games of all time, with a large and active community of players and modders.
